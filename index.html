🥇 1st Prize

Android Development Ideathon

This achievement was earned for the RentEase AI idea.

What was the idea?

RentEase AI is an AI-powered rental platform designed to help users find suitable rental houses, apartments, PGs, hostels and rooms based on their location, budget and preferences.

What problem does it address?

Finding suitable rental accommodation can be difficult because users need to compare location, budget, property type and nearby services.

What does RentEase AI provide?

  • AI-powered recommendations
  • Rental property discovery
  • Location-based search
  • Nearby services
  • Maps integration
  • Property listings
  • Ratings and reviews
